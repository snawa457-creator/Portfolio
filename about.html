A Little About Me

My interests have always extended beyond one particular field. I enjoy the technical side of programming and problem-solving, but I'm equally interested in design, business, and the creative process behind developing new ideas.

Studying Computer Information Systems has allowed me to explore these interests together. Through my coursework, I've gained experience with different programming languages, web development, and information systems, while my extracurricular involvement has introduced me to entrepreneurship, innovation, and project management.

One of the things I enjoy most about university is having the opportunity to explore new interests. I've participated in business competitions, joined student organizations, attended professional development events, and worked on collaborative technical projects.

These experiences have helped me develop a better understanding of my strengths, the areas I want to improve, and the different career opportunities available within technology.

As I continue my degree, I hope to build a stronger technical foundation while remaining involved in opportunities that challenge me to think creatively, collaborate with others, and continue learning.
